## Haggag result

The minimum coherent reconstruction contains **123 drills**. At the displayed
configured cost of **1.35 condition/run**, that is **166.05** expected condition
loss versus **166** observed across the three condition blocks.

Among **102 clean single-run transitions**, 69 display -1 and 33 display -2.
P(-2)=0.3235; the 95% Wilson interval is [0.2406,0.4193], and the exact
two-sided test against 0.35 gives p=0.6054.

This is compatible with stochastic/quantized integer settlement around the
fractional mean. A single conserved fractional carry is falsified by the
ordered sequence, including adjacent -2/-2 outcomes.

Stats end +21 overall (Tackling +5, Dribbling +8, Finishing +8), while only
19 +1 points are directly rendered in captured reports. Constant carry is also
falsified for Dribbling by the observed gain-gap sequence.

poor and this is not fresh prospective evidence.

## Consequence

The screenshot experiment changes our interpretation, but not in the naive way.

Fractional state is real and should remain an observation-layer consideration.
It can explain small display-level perturbations and should prevent us from
over-interpreting ±1 endpoint differences as new physics.

It does **not** explain the majority of Resource Coach residuals. The remaining
error is upstream of rendering: response shape, allocation/dose, or another
player/coach state variable remains missing.

The next useful analysis is therefore residual-structure identification *after*
marginalising the small integer-renderer uncertainty, rather than further
rounding/carry variants.
