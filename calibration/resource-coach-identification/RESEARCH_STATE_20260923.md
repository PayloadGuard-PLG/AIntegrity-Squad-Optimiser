## Independent calculations and attempted falsification

The replay fits **both** observed low and high endpoints, allowing each an explicit ±0.5 display-rounding tolerance. It never substitutes interval midpoints. Its competing cost surfaces are flat, smooth exponential, and flat-below-threshold/exponential-above-threshold. Dose regression is deliberately descriptive: intercept, age, log multiplier, log affected count, class, programme and coach shape. The fitted exponents are correlated with programme, shape and player composition, and are **not** recovered source-game parameters. Seven model comparisons, full errors and worst misses are in [replay-20260923.json](replay-20260923.json).

| Cost response, class treatment | 132-row leave-one-player-out overlap | Mean nonoverlap gap | Leave-one-session-out overlap | 46 independent workbook intervals overlap |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Flat, raw stat | 35.6% | 12.11 | 43.2% | 26.1% |
| Flat, tier-adjusted WHITE | 34.8% | 12.22 | 44.7% | 26.1% |
| Threshold 135/120, slope .015, tier-adjusted | 50.0% | 2.67 | 63.6% | 32.6% |
| Threshold 135/120, slope .0354, tier-adjusted | **90.2%** | **0.60** | **89.4%** | **41.3%** |
| Threshold 135/120, slope .0354, raw stat | 50.8% | 7.06 | 54.5% | 23.9% |
| Common threshold 135, slope .0354, tier-adjusted | 82.6% | 0.81 | 87.1% | 34.8% |
| Smooth exponential, slope .0354, tier-adjusted | 71.2% | 8.70 | 76.5% | 30.4% |

The archive result supports a *family of tier-adjusted threshold responses* over these tested alternatives, but does not prove thresholds 135/120 or slope .0354. At threshold 135 with slopes .025/.0354/.045, held-out overlap is 84.1%/90.2%/78.8%; the full nine-point threshold/slope grid is in the replay. Thresholds at 100 and 170 generally do worse, although threshold 100/slope .025 still overlaps 81.8% and cannot be ruled out by aggregate coverage alone. The fitted diagnostic q/eta for the .0354 model are about 1.02/1.01; across plausible cost assumptions they change materially. The 46-row cross-source result is only 19/46 overlap and is especially poor for ordinary Standard Physical/Defending previews (multiple misses of 20–34 points). It is an imperfect family-transfer test, yet a decisive warning against implementation from retrospective fit. Within archive, Neri's Training Camp and Scott-related previews remain systematic misses. The apparently perfect 60/60 Drill Session leave-one-player-out interval overlap is helped by wide historic intervals and is not validation for Skill Seminar.

**Exact and near controls.** The disputed Darren Moore Extensive All-Round x57/x114 pair matches player state, coach, affected seven stats and family on paper. With literal endpoints, flat cost forces a common dose ratio to be at least 39/19=2.053 from Passing but exactly 16/8=2 from Positioning. With ±0.5 display tolerance, flat cost becomes feasible at dose ratio 1.974–2.102. Under the *proposed* thresholds/slope .0354 and even a full ±1 stat tolerance, the seven transformed cost-ratio intervals have empty intersection (limiting lower 3.172, upper 2.935). This conditionally falsifies fixed per-stat allocation with that response surface; **it is not trusted source-game falsification until `61747.png` is reconciled.** An extrapolation of the old Academy geometric decay δ=.99 would yield x114/x57 dose ratio 1+.99^57=1.564, below even the pair's rounding-aware minimum, but the original Academy law does not claim to govern Resource Coach. The only currently usable exact multiplier controls are 24 archived stat pairs for Willie Howden, Lt Ripley and Garry McCluskey, each same state/coach/family at x106/x114; their interval constraints are too wide to identify q. For the nominal response their three preview-wide ratio intersections are about [0.726,1.567], [0.762,1.493], [0.775,1.369]. Within previews, 212 archived and 55 undisputed canonical same-class starting-stat order comparisons at nonnegative latent coordinates have **zero strict gain-order inversions**; the four archived and two undisputed canonical equal-start/same-class pairs also have no disjoint gains. That supports a monotone common response but does not identify equal allocation. Howden x106 Training Camp and Drill Session have identical reported intervals in the secondary source, without establishing a universal family equality. The canonical ordinary Standard Attacking x30 trio changes both player/roles and starting stat, so it is a near control, not a role or marginal-cost isolation. No trusted canonical exact isolated multiplier contrast survives the quarantine; the updated automated analyser correctly reports **zero** exact cancellation pairs.

**OVR.** Exact equality `OVR gain interval = sum(affected gain intervals)/15` is disjoint in 9/22 distinct historical previews. Every one of the 22 is compatible with an integer displayed-difference envelope from `floor(sum_lo/15)` to `ceil(sum_hi/15)`, allowing an unknown fractional starting OVR. Thus aggregate/15 plus display rounding remains plausible, but neither exact equality nor an endpoint renderer is identified. No hard stat cap or threshold discontinuity is established: Neri's T6 WHITE Tackling 135 has latent coordinate −25 and visible [0,0] while WHITE Fitness 260 has +[16,24]; a movement below 25 can be fully hidden by rectification, and steep cost/rounding can also explain high-start zero gains. The 13 September report's claim that this pair alone falsifies shared budget overlooked rectification.
